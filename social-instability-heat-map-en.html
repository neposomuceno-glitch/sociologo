Social Instability Heat Map

A sourced, composite snapshot of where entrenched versus emerging instability is currently concentrated — built from published indices, not opinion.

How colors were assigned. Each country's level reflects its standing across three current, cited sources: the Fragile States Index 2024/25 (Fund for Peace — a composite score of security, economy, human rights, cohesion), the FAO/WFP Hunger Hotspots 2026 report (highest/very high concern levels for acute food insecurity), and the ACLED Conflict Watchlist / active conflict monitoring.

Red = entrenched, overlapping crises (e.g. active war + famine risk + a top-15 FSI score). Orange = a serious, ongoing crisis in a single dimension, or mid-level fragility. Yellow = an emerging or localized concern flagged in at least one index, without the pileup seen in red/orange.

This is a snapshot sourced as of September 2026; all of these indices update on their own schedule, so treat this as a starting point and consult the primary source for anything time-sensitive (see the maps subpage for direct links).

Red — entrenched / overlapping Orange — serious, one major crisis Yellow — emerging / localized concern

What this isn't: a real-time source. Compare against the live sources on the maps subpage — the Fragile States Index heat map, ACLED, FEWS NET/IPC, the Global Peace Index, the Global Organized Crime Index — for anything current or decision-relevant.

Coverage gaps: this snapshot favors countries appearing near the top of at least one major index; many countries with moderate, non-headline instability are not listed and should not be read as "stable" by omission.

On disputed cases: entries here (e.g. Gaza/Palestine, Myanmar, Ethiopia) reflect what the cited indices report, not a political stance — consult the primary source if a rating will be used for anything consequential.
