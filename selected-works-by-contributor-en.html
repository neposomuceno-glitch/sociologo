Selected Works, by Contributor

A companion to Key Contributors to Understanding Social Problems — the principal works of each person listed there, in chronological order where more than one is given. Titles link to a Google Books search for that exact title (a reliable, non-breaking linking method for around 100 individual titles) rather than to a specific edition or publisher page; use that link to find whichever edition, translation, or library copy you want. Titles are kept in their original language of publication.

Classical foundations

  • Karl Marx — Das Kapital (Capital) (1867); The Communist Manifesto (with Friedrich Engels, 1848)
  • Émile Durkheim — The Division of Labour in Society (1893); Suicide: A Study in Sociology (1897); The Elementary Forms of Religious Life (1912)
  • Max Weber — The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism (1905); Economy and Society (published posthumously, 1922)
  • W.E.B. Du Bois — The Philadelphia Negro (1899); The Souls of Black Folk (1903); Black Reconstruction in America (1935)
  • Jane Addams — Twenty Years at Hull-House (1910); Democracy and Social Ethics (1902)
  • Robert E. Park — The City (with Ernest Burgess, 1925); Introduction to the Science of Sociology (with Burgess, 1921)
  • Robert K. Merton — Social Theory and Social Structure (1949, expanded 1968)
  • C. Wright Mills — The Power Elite (1956); The Sociological Imagination (1959)
  • Erving Goffman — The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life (1956); Asylums (1961); Stigma: Notes on the Management of Spoiled Identity (1963)
  • Howard Becker — Outsiders: Studies in the Sociology of Deviance (1963)

Non-Western and Global South foundations

  • Ibn Khaldun — The Muqaddimah (Introduction to Universal History) (1377), part of his broader universal history Kitab al-'Ibar
  • Fei Xiaotong — Peasant Life in China (1939); From the Soil (1947, translated 1992)
  • Orlando Fals Borda — Knowledge and People's Power (1985); co-editor of Action and Knowledge: Breaking the Monopoly with Participatory Action-Research (1991)

Race, poverty, and urban inequality

  • William Julius Wilson — The Declining Significance of Race (1978); The Truly Disadvantaged (1987); When Work Disappears (1996)
  • Elijah Anderson — Streetwise (1990); Code of the Street (1999)
  • Loïc Wacquant — Urban Outcasts (2008); Punishing the Poor (2009)
  • Matthew Desmond — Evicted: Poverty and Profit in the American City (2016); Poverty, by America (2023)
  • Devah Pager — Marked: Race, Crime, and Finding Work in an Era of Mass Incarceration (2007)
  • Herbert Gans — The Urban Villagers (1962); "The Uses of Poverty: The Poor Pay All" (essay, Social Policy, 1971)

Global inequality, development, and world-systems

  • Immanuel Wallerstein — The Modern World-System (4 volumes, 1974-2011); World-Systems Analysis: An Introduction (2004)

Deviance, medicalization, crime, and justice

  • Peter Conrad — Identifying Hyperactive Children: The Medicalization of Deviant Behavior (1976, expanded 2006); The Medicalization of Society (2007)

Gender, intersectionality, and family

  • Patricia Hill Collins — Black Feminist Thought (1990); Intersectionality (with Sirma Bilge, 2016)

Solution-oriented / civic-capital theorists

  • Nan Lin — Social Capital: A Theory of Social Structure and Action (2001)
  • Pierre Bourdieu — Outline of a Theory of Practice (1972); Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste (1979)

Digital sociology and the network society

  • Manuel Castells — The Rise of the Network Society (1996, Vol. I of the trilogy The Information Age); The Power of Identity (1997, Vol. II); End of Millennium (1998, Vol. III)

Notes

  • Links go to a Google Books search for the exact title rather than a single fixed edition, since most of these titles have multiple editions, translations, and publishers in circulation — a search lets you pick whichever you want (library copy, specific translation, current edition in print) rather than being limited to one.
  • Where a figure's central contribution is a single essay or article rather than a book (Gans's "Uses of Poverty"), that is noted in place of a monograph link — it is typically anthologized in later collections if the original journal isn't accessible to you.
  • This list maps one-to-one to the contributors page; if a name is added there, their works should be added here too.
